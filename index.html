A BUSINESS THAT GROWS WITH TRUST

Our approach

Useful plant care first. Revenue should support the experience, not get in its way.

OUR STARTING POINT

Keep care free. Charge for extra peace of mind.

Start with a useful free reminder and collection. Offer an optional paid plan for richer care histories, shared household access, and travel planning. Add a sitter marketplace only after local demand and safety can be proven.

01

Free essentials

Watering schedules, a manageable plant collection, and basic reminders help people build a habit before asking them to pay.

Build trust
02

Optional Sprout Plus

A modest subscription could unlock shared care, unlimited history, seasonal guidance, and trip checklists. Test willingness to pay first.

Recurring revenue
03

Sitter bookings, later

Once a neighborhood has enough vetted sitters, a transparent fee per completed booking could create value for both sides.

Two-sided marketplace
Why not ads first?

Display ads can distract from a quiet care routine, weaken trust, and usually need lots of traffic to earn much. Keep the product ad-free while testing; reconsider only if customers prefer sponsored plant products and those can be clearly labeled.

Student prototype: subscriptions, sitter profiles, and bookings are concepts only. No real accounts, payments, or services are provided.
